Mandiant describes BONESAW as a Go utility primarily built for remote exploitation. Its reported capabilities include extracting an arbitrary Lightning Memory-Mapped Database, hijacking the SAML Security Token Service, and creating an SSO Administrator account. These are capabilities attributed to the utility; the supplied excerpt does not establish that every capability was exercised in the observed intrusion.
