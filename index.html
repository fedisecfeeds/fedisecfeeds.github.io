- Hit <Ok>

!mozilla/emSign_ECC_Root_CA_-_C3.crt

!mozilla/emSign_Root_CA_-_C1.crt
!mozilla/emSign_Root_CA_-_G1.crt
!mozilla/Comodo_AAA_Services_root.crt

...and sudo update-ca-certificates
